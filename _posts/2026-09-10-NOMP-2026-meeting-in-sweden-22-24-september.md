The yearly meeting of the Nordic Marine Phytoplankton group (NOMP) will be held in Gothenburg, Sweden 22-24 September. SMHI (Swedish Meteorological and Hydrological Institute) is hosting the meeting. There will be 34 participants representing all countries in NOMP: Sweden, Norway, Iceland, Faroe Islands, Denmark and Greenland. The 31 presentations will range from research project on phytoplankton, monitoring and HABs to imaging instruments, species lists and various problems that occur when you analyze microscopy samples. In summary, three days where we together dive into the wonderful world of phytoplankton!
